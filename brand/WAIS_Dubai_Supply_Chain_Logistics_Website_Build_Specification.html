02Brand kit & design direction

The visual identity projects an authoritative, high-density, luminous trade infrastructure aesthetic engineered for freight decision-makers: Chief Supply Chain Officers, Port Authority Directors, Terminal Operations Chiefs, and Fleet Heads.

DoDeep obsidian canvas (#020814), glass surfaces, clean monospace tags, high-contrast Cyber Lime CTA pills with Carbon Ink, and verified trade benchmarks.
AvoidMaritime hardware trade show visuals, diesel engines, rate broking directories, speculative logistics hype, and artificial countdown timers.

Rule — Button Contrast

Never place white text inside a Cyber Lime (#C0F43C) button or badge. Typography inside Cyber Lime elements must strictly use Carbon Ink (#06111F !important).
